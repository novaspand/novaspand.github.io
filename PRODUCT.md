# PRODUCT.md — Novaspand

## Register

Brand. This is a studio/company landing page where design IS the product. First impression is everything. The site must communicate credibility and distinctiveness before a single word is read.

## Product

Novaspand is a multi-product tech studio — not locked into one category. ERP systems, Laravel packages, open-source tools, and whatever solves a real problem next. A tech lab at its core, client-facing in its presentation.

## Target Users

**Primary:** Potential clients evaluating whether to hire or partner with Novaspand. They are decision-makers — founders, project leads, ops managers — who can smell generic from a mile away. They need to feel "these people are serious" and "this is different from other tech companies" within the first three seconds.

**Secondary:** Developers and builders in the ecosystem who may collaborate, use open-source tools, or follow the studio's work.

## Brand Personality

Three real words: **Grounded. Precise. Unexpected.**

Not aggressive. Not playful. Confidently smart — the kind of studio that makes you pause for half a second before it clicks. Warm enough to feel human. Serious enough to earn trust immediately.

The name is the signal: Nava (Sanskrit — new) + Spanda (Sanskrit — the creative pulse, the vibration behind all creation). Innovation baked in without screaming it.

## Brand Voice

- Short sentences. No filler.
- Statements, not promises.
- No "boost your productivity." No "we help you grow."
- Speak like a studio that has already done the work, not one selling you on doing it.
- Warm but never casual. Serious but never cold.

## Design Principles

1. **Space is confidence.** Generous whitespace signals that nothing needs to fight for attention.
2. **Every element earns its place.** If it cannot justify itself, it does not exist on the page.
3. **Restraint is the aesthetic.** One accent color, used sparingly. Type does the heavy lifting.
4. **Slow is premium.** Motion is deliberate and weighted. Nothing bounces. Nothing spins.
5. **Light exposes quality.** The light theme is a choice that demands precision — there is nowhere to hide.

## References

- High-end architecture studio websites (spare, typographic, authoritative)
- Editorial design — think serious print magazines, not blogs
- Pentagram, Wolff Olins studio presence
- Premium consultancy identity (not flashy, just unmistakably serious)

## Anti-References

- Purple gradients on dark backgrounds
- Glassmorphism
- Generic SaaS landing pages ("The all-in-one platform for your team")
- Startup template layouts — hero with illustration, three feature cards, pricing table
- Bright neon tech aesthetics
- Cards on cards on cards
- Inter font used everywhere
- Animated counters ("10,000+ happy customers")
- Stock photography of people at laptops

## Accessibility

- High contrast text on light backgrounds — no low-contrast muted body copy
- Readable at all sizes — Libre Baskerville and Mulish must both render cleanly at small sizes
- Motion should respect prefers-reduced-motion

## Site Structure

1. Hero — who Novaspand is, one strong declarative line
2. What we do — studio scope, types of work
3. Why Novaspand — values and edge, what makes the studio different
4. Work / Products — what has been built or is being built
5. Contact — simple, no friction
